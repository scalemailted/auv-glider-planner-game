# Operational Depth Slabs and Continuous Sampling

Operational slabs are analysis and visualization references. They are not physical floors, route waypoints, or station-keeping commands.

Canonical glider depth is continuous. When the glider lies between operational layers, deterministic depth interpolation reports the adjacent layers and interpolation fraction, and samples A(x,y,z,t) at actual z.

At the same x/y, surface, shallow, thermocline, midwater, and deep values may differ. Correlated fields may resemble each other, but layer differences must come from canonical source fields or deterministic derived summaries, not display tinting.

Target layers guide profile selection and sampling intent. They cannot award a deep sample when the glider remained near the surface.
